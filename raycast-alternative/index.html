Raycast is a polished launcher with an extension store and AI built in. Copperlight covers the everyday core, from apps and files to clipboard history, snippets and windows, with no account and no AI service, and every line of it is open source.

Which one is for you

Pick Raycast if

You want extensions from its Store for the services you use, AI built in, or the same launcher on Windows and iPhone.

Pick Copperlight if

You want a launcher you can read every line of, settings in one text file, and nothing to sign in to. Script commands you wrote for Raycast come with you.

Moving over from Raycast

  • Hotkey. Copperlight opens with ⌥Space. If Raycast uses it, give one of them another key in its Settings, or quit Raycast.
  • Script commands. Copy them into ~/.config/copperlight/scripts/. Their @raycast headers, title, mode, description, arguments and confirmation, are read as they are.
  • Quicklinks become commands in config.toml, with {query} where your text goes: 
    [[command]]
alias = "gh"
type  = "url"
url   = "https://github.com/search?q={query}"
  • Snippets become [[snippet]] entries, with {date}, {time}, {clipboard} and {query} filled in, and {cursor} for where the cursor goes.
  • Extensions from the Raycast Store don’t run in Copperlight. Many everyday ones are built in: clipboard history, emoji, translation, timers, a pomodoro and window commands.

Questions

Is there a free, open source alternative to Raycast?

Yes. Copperlight is free and open source under the GPL-3.0. It covers apps, files, clipboard history, snippets, window management, math and conversions, with no account.

Do Raycast script commands work in Copperlight?

Yes. Put them in Copperlight’s scripts folder and their @raycast headers are read as they are. Extensions from the Raycast Store don’t run in Copperlight.

Can I use Copperlight without an account?

Yes. Copperlight has no accounts at all, and no telemetry.

Does Copperlight have AI like Raycast?

No. Copperlight has no AI service and won’t add one. With Apple Intelligence on, the Mac’s own model helps it find apps by what they do.

Can Copperlight and Raycast run side by side?

Yes, as long as each has its own hotkey.
